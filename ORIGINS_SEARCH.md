# Sudoku Mine Origin Search

## Topline

- Current working conclusion: the earliest known **exact** `9x9` / `3 mines in every row, column, and 3x3 box` version located so far is **Cross+A** in **late 2012**. That version is understood to be **copied from an unknown earlier original**.
- The format was also invented independently by:
  - **chaotic_iak**, who published a **2-mine jigsaw version** in **2011** and has confirmed this was an independent invention.
  - **James Dewar (JDK)** in **2024**.
- Related puzzles combining **Sudoku** and **Minesweeper-style clue mechanics** appear to go back to at least **2006**, although those earlier examples are not yet the exact `9x9 / 3-mine` format.
- In this note, the earliest **concretely dated** related examples pinned so far are **May 4, 2007** (`3 Star Sudoku`) and **March 14, 2010** (`Minesweeper Sudoku` in Logic Masters India).

## Exact Format: What Is Confirmed So Far

- **Cross+A / Ilya Morozov**: earliest known public lead so far for the exact modern format is **late 2012**.
- Cross+A is **not believed to be the original source**; it is treated as a copied version from an unknown earlier puzzle.
- Ilya Morozov recalled that he may have seen the puzzle on the now-defunct German site **Sachsentext.de** by **Uwe Wiedemann**, likely around **2012**, but archival searching has **not yet confirmed** the exact puzzle there.

## Independent Reinventions

- **chaotic_iak** published related **Minesweeper (Sudoku)** puzzles in **2011**:
  - archive page: [chaoticiak.github.io/logic.html](https://chaoticiak.github.io/logic.html#minesweepersudoku-2011-03-bmpc1-8)
  - these use essentially the same hybrid idea, but with a **jigsaw / irregular-region** structure and a **2-mine** version rather than the exact `9x9 / 3-mine` format.
- **James Dewar (JDK)** independently created the format in **2024**.

## Earlier Related Sudoku + Minesweeper Hybrids

- **Logic Masters India**, **March 14, 2010**:
  - a puzzle explicitly called **Minesweeper Sudoku** appears in the **Sudoku Mock Test 16** instruction booklet.
  - page: [logicmastersindia.com/mock16](https://logicmastersindia.com/mock16/)
  - PDF: [sudokumocktest16ib.pdf](https://logicmastersindia.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/sudokumocktest16ib.pdf)
- **Sachsentext.de** precursor formats:
  - **3 Star Sudoku**, archived **May 4, 2007**: [archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20070504185101/http://www.sachsentext.de:80/en/3star1.htm)
  - **Stardust Sudoku**, visible in archive by **September 29, 2010**: [archive image](https://web.archive.org/web/20100929075130/http://sachsentext.de/gif/stardust_sudoku1.png)
  - these are important precursors because they use the idea of placing a fixed number of marked cells in Sudoku structures, but they are **not** the exact Minesweeper-clue format.
- **Sudoku Xtra** shows that Minesweeper-style puzzle ideas were actively discussed in Sudoku communities by **2009-2010**:
  - **November 19, 2009**: [New puzzles for issue 2](https://www.sudokuxtra.com/forum/read.php?2,16) refers to `Mosaic` as essentially Minesweeper with pictures.
  - **January 3, 2010**: [Community Puzzles](https://www.sudokuxtra.com/forum/read.php?2,91) describes `Big Foot` as a "minesweeper-like" idea.
  - **February 25, 2010**: [Sudoku Xtra Issue 4 is now online!](https://www.sudokuxtra.com/forum/read.php?2,207) lists `Mosaic (Minesweeper picture puzzle)`.

  - searches for obvious names and descriptions such as `Minensudoku`, `Minesweeper Sudoku`, `Minedoku`, and `3 Minen` did **not** produce a confirmed hit for the exact puzzle.

## Best Remaining Leads

- **Sudoku World** (`Yahoo Groups`) remains a strong candidate because it was cited elsewhere as an early source of variant ideas, but it is difficult to search in surviving public archives.
- **forsmarts** also remains worth checking more deeply for the same reason.
- The most important open question is still:
  - **Where is the earliest documented exact `9x9 / 3-mine` version before Cross+A in late 2012?**
